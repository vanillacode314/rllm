# What's New

## v0.0.20

### ✨ What's New

- **Choose where you land** — Pick what opens when you launch the app: your last chat, a fresh chat, or the scratchpad (Settings → General)
- **New General settings page** — A home for app-wide preferences, so launch behavior and reasoning display are easy to find

### 🎨 Improvements

- **Tidier document library** — Document cards look cleaner, and upload progress now shows a rounded percentage

### ⚠️ Note on a behavior change

- The reasoning panel is now **hidden by default** while a response is being generated. If you liked seeing it live, turn off *"Hide Reasoning During Generation"* in Settings → General.
