# What's New

## v0.0.14 — August 7, 2026

### ✨ What's New

- **Document library** — attach a PDF or EPUB once and it's saved to your library, so you can reuse it in any chat without re-uploading. Files are indexed in the background with a progress indicator, and you can browse, search, and delete them anytime from the new Documents page.
- **Pick documents from your library** — the attach menu now includes a "Library" option, letting you pull in any previously indexed document for a conversation.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Failed responses stay visible** — if the last AI response fails, reloading the chat no longer makes the error disappear. You can now see what went wrong and retry.
